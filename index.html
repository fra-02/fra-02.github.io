Research on the effect of emotion prompts on ChatGPT-4 answer quality across 25 technical Stack Overflow questions. Three types of prompts (neutral + 2 emotional) were evaluated using BLEU, ROUGE, BERTScore, sentiment analysis, and code reviews.
